Our Story

Born from a love of health and wellness.

Constitution Fitness was founded by Nick Mongillo, a local pediatrician who has always believed that a strong community starts with strong, healthy people. His practice, Pedicare, has cared for Shelton-area families for years upstairs — and when the space below the office opened up, Nick saw the perfect opportunity to bring that same passion for wellness to adults.

What started as an idea became a boutique studio in the basement of Pedicare: a tight-knit space for spin, yoga, and strength work, built by neighbors, for neighbors.
